THE 54D HEALTH BASELINE

The right markers for your body.

Men and women do not receive an identical panel. Your baseline combines core health markers with sex-specific testing selected for a more relevant clinical picture.

Men’s baseline

  • CBC
  • Comprehensive Metabolic Panel
  • HbA1c
  • Lipid Panel
  • TSH

Women’s baseline

  • CBC
  • Comprehensive Metabolic Panel
  • HbA1c
  • Lipid Panel
  • TSH
  • Ferritin

PLUS  • Blood Pressure  • Resting Heart Rate  • Weight  • BMI  • Waist Circumference

Final test selection may vary based on sex, medical history and the physician’s clinical judgment.

54D CHANGES

METABOLIC CLARITY

“I’m training consistently, but my weight and energy aren’t responding.”

Your results give your doctor a clearer starting point to discuss personalized metabolic care, including prescription weight-management options when clinically appropriate.

RECOVERY & PERFORMANCE

“I’m working hard, but I’m constantly tired and recovering slowly.”

If your results and medical history support it, your doctor can discuss targeted recovery options, including peptide-based therapies when clinically appropriate.

HORMONAL HEALTH

“I thought the changes I was feeling were simply part of getting older.”

Your doctor can evaluate your hormone markers and discuss hormone therapy or other treatment options when clinically appropriate.

Treatment eligibility is determined by a licensed physician. Medications and treatments, if prescribed, are not included in the program price.
